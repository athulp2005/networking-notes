# Types of Networks

## Overview

Networks can be classified based on the geographical area they cover and how they are used.

The main types of networks are:

- PAN
- LAN
- MAN
- WAN
- WLAN

---

## PAN — Personal Area Network

PAN stands for **Personal Area Network**.

It is used to connect personal devices within a small area around an individual.

### Example

A smartwatch connected to a smartphone.

    Smartphone ↔ Smartwatch

---

## LAN — Local Area Network

LAN stands for **Local Area Network**.

It is used to connect devices within a small geographical area such as a home, office, school, or building.

### Example

An office network connecting computers and other devices.

    PC ──┐
    PC ──┼── LAN
    PC ──┘

---

## MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

MAN stands for **Metropolitan Area Network**.

It connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.

### Example

Networks connecting different locations within a city.

    Office A ──┐
    Office B ──┼── MAN
    Office C ──┘

---

## WAN — Wide Area Network

WAN stands for **Wide Area Network**.

It connects networks across large geographical areas such as different cities, countries, or continents.

### Example

The Internet is an example of a WAN.

    Network A ──┐
    Network B ──┼── WAN
    Network C ──┘

---

## WLAN — Wireless Local Area Network

WLAN stands for **Wireless Local Area Network**.

It is a local network that uses wireless communication, usually through Wi-Fi.

### Example

A home Wi-Fi network.

    Wi-Fi Router
       /  |  \
    Laptop Phone TV

---

## Comparison

| Type | Full Form | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| PAN | Personal Area Network | Smartwatch + Phone |
| LAN | Local Area Network | Office Network |
| MAN | Metropolitan Area Network | City Connection |
| WAN | Wide Area Network | Internet |
| WLAN | Wireless Local Area Network | Home Wi-Fi |

---

## Easy Way to Remember

- **PAN** → Personal
- **LAN** → Local
- **MAN** → Metropolitan
- **WAN** → Wide
- **WLAN** → Wireless Local

---

## Key Points

- **PAN** connects personal devices.
- **LAN** connects devices within a local area.
- **MAN** connects networks across a city.
- **WAN** connects networks across large geographical areas.
- **WLAN** provides wireless local networking.
- The Internet is an example of WAN.
- Home Wi-Fi is an example of WLAN.

---

## Summary

PAN, LAN, MAN, WAN, and WLAN are different types of networks based mainly on their geographical coverage and method of connection.

Understanding these network types is important for learning how networks are designed and how devices communicate across different areas.
