# What is Networking?

## 📖 Overview

Networking is the process of connecting two or more devices so they can communicate and share information. These devices may include computers, laptops, mobile phones, printers, servers, and other network-enabled devices.

A network allows users to exchange data, access shared resources, and communicate efficiently.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After completing this topic, you should be able to:

- Understand what networking is.
- Explain why networking is important.
- Identify devices that can be connected in a network.
- Understand the basic purpose of computer networks.

---

## 🌐 What is Networking?

Networking is the interconnection of two or more devices to enable communication and the sharing of resources.

The connected devices exchange information through communication channels using networking technologies.

---

## 💻 Devices in a Network

Examples of devices that can be connected include:

- Computers
- Laptops
- Smartphones
- Servers
- Printers
- Routers
- Switches

---

## 🎯 Purpose of Networking

Networking is used to:

- Share information
- Share resources
- Enable communication
- Access centralized services
- Connect devices over short or long distances

---

## 🌍 Real-World Example

In a school or office, multiple computers are connected through a network so users can:

- Access shared files
- Use the same printer
- Communicate with each other
- Access the internet

---

## 💡 Key Points

- Networking connects multiple devices.
- Connected devices can communicate with each other.
- Networks allow sharing of data and resources.
- Networking is used in homes, schools, offices, and businesses.

---

## 📌 Summary

Networking is the foundation of modern communication. By connecting devices together, it becomes possible to share information, resources, and services efficiently. Understanding networking basics is the first step toward learning advanced networking and cybersecurity concepts.
